The Process

From Inquiry to Delivery

A clear, repeatable process designed for efficiency and trust.

01

Receive & Verify Inquiry

You submit a code-based inquiry or upload a BOM. We verify the codes against our LIA product database (HASCO / Meusburger compatible) and confirm specifications within 24 hours.

02

Direct Quote, No Comparison

You submit requirements. We provide the best price and lead time directly. No waiting for multiple quotes, no self-comparison. One request, one response.

03

Sample & Approval

For new parts or new customers, we arrange sample production first. Samples are inspected against spec before shipment. You approve — then we proceed to full order.

04

Production & QC Inspection

We monitor production milestones and conduct in-house or third-party QC inspection before goods leave the factory. Every batch is assigned a LIA traceability lot number.

05

Export & International Delivery

We handle all export documentation (packing list, commercial invoice, COO, material certificate). Parts ship DDP or DAP to your European / global warehouse.

06

After-Sales & Reorder

Confirmed suppliers and approved specs are locked in your account. Reorders take hours, not days. Any quality issue is resolved within one business cycle.
