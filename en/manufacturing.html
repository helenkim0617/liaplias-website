What Manufacturing Capabilities Do We Offer?

Precision Mold Components

Mold Cores, Inserts, Slides & Lifters

Materials

What Materials Can We Work With?

We work with manufacturing partners experienced in a range of metals, alloys and engineering plastics, selected according to the application and technical requirements.

Tool Steels

For mold components, tooling and high-wear applications.

Stainless Steels

For corrosion-resistant and precision components.

Aluminum Alloys

For lightweight components requiring dimensional accuracy and efficient machining.

Copper Alloys

For applications requiring thermal or electrical conductivity.

Engineering Plastics

For injection-molded and precision plastic components.

Specialty Materials

Additional material grades can be considered according to the specific application and manufacturing requirements.

Not sure which material fits your application? Send your requirement and we can review the appropriate options.

Quality Assurance

Manufacturing Quality

Manufacturing quality begins with a clear technical understanding of the requirement, and continues through agreed specifications and monitored production.

01

Engineering Review

Every requirement is reviewed against the available technical information, including drawings, specifications, materials and tolerances where applicable, to confirm manufacturability before production begins.

02

Supply Network →

How It Works

What Happens After I Send an Inquiry?

A clear process turns your technical requirement into a practical manufacturing solution.

01

Send Your Requirement

Share your requirement — a drawing, STEP file, BOM or project description.

02

Engineering Review

Our team reviews the technical information and confirms key requirements, clarifying details where needed.

03

Manufacturing Match

Your requirement is matched with the appropriate manufacturing capability within our network, based on your project's current requirements.

04

Receive a Quote

Based on the reviewed specifications, you receive a quotation covering pricing, lead time and production terms.

05

Production & Delivery

Once approved, production proceeds under agreed specifications and monitored quality requirements, with inspection, documentation and shipment coordinated to your specified destination.
