Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

LIAPLIAS ("we", "us", "our") operates www.liaplias.com as a precision manufacturing sourcing and inquiry platform connecting buyers with qualified manufacturing partners. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

We collect two kinds of information:

  • Information you provide directly — for example, your name, company name, email address, message, and any file you attach when you submit an Inquiry, RFQ, or BOM upload through our contact forms.
  • Information collected automatically — basic technical and usage data such as your approximate location (country/region), browser type, device type, pages visited, and referring source, collected through website analytics tools.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data, and our forms are intended for business (B2B) inquiries rather than for use by consumers.

2. How We Use Inquiry Form Data

Data submitted through our Inquiry, RFQ, or Contact forms is used for the following purposes:

  • to respond to your inquiry and prepare quotations or technical clarifications;
  • to identify the relevant parts, technical references, or cross-reference codes, and pricing you requested;
  • to maintain a record of our business communications with you, where reasonably necessary for order handling and after-sales support.

We do not sell your data to third parties, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your consent where consent is required by applicable law. We may share relevant inquiry information with qualified manufacturing partners within our Supply Network where necessary to evaluate manufacturing feasibility, prepare quotations, or fulfill an order.

3. Cookies and Analytics

We use website analytics tools to understand overall traffic and how visitors use the site — for example, which pages are viewed and which categories or SKUs are searched. This data is used in aggregate to improve site content and navigation and is not intended to identify individual visitors personally.

Analytics tools may set cookies or similar technologies in your browser. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time; doing so should not prevent you from browsing the site, though some interactive features (such as the RFQ list) rely on temporary in-browser data to function during your visit.

4. Data Retention and Security

We retain inquiry and communication records for as long as reasonably necessary for the business purposes described above, or as required by applicable law. Inquiry records may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for quotation history, contractual records, dispute resolution, or compliance purposes, subject to applicable law. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information you share with us, though no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

5. Third-Party Trademarks Referenced on This Site

Product codes, technical references, and cross-reference information (including references to third-party brands where applicable) are provided for identification and compatibility purposes only and do not imply any data-sharing relationship between LIAPLIAS and those companies. See our Terms of Use for more on trademarks.

6. Your Choices

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information that you have provided to us. You may also ask us to stop contacting you for non-essential communications, at any time, by emailing us at the address below.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our website or business practices evolve. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, contact us at info@liaplias.com.
