Mold Standard Components

LIAPLIAS supplies a comprehensive catalog of high-precision mold standard components engineered for 1:1 interchangeability with HASCO and Meusburger systems. Our product lineup includes sprue bushings, return pins, ball guide pillars, self-lubricating graphite bushings, and temperature regulation fittings. Designed to withstand high-pressure injection molding, die casting, and elevated thermal cycles, our components ensure high durability and smooth continuous operation.
