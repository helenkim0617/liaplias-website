Solutions

Three Solutions. One Brand.

LIAPLIAS is built around three structural advantages — independence from single-platform dependency, localized after-sales support, and dedicated key account management for high-volume customers.

01
Platform Independence

Your Supply Chain. Not Tied to Any Platform.

LIAPLIAS operates as a direct brand — not a marketplace, not a broker. Your orders, specifications, and pricing agreements are maintained directly with us. No platform dependency, no algorithmic repricing, no risk of supplier substitution without your knowledge.

✓

Direct brand relationship — you deal with LIAPLIAS directly, not through a third-party platform that can change terms or delist products.

✓

Locked specifications — approved part specs, material grades, and supplier assignments are fixed in your account. No substitutions without your sign-off.

✓

LIA code ownership — your LIA part numbers belong to your procurement system. They map to stable specifications regardless of market changes.

✓

Transparent pricing structure — unit price, tooling cost, and freight are itemized separately. No bundled fees or hidden markups.

Why Independence Matters

Most industrial buyers have experienced the risk of platform dependency — a supplier changes, a product gets relisted, pricing shifts without notice. LIAPLIAS is structured to eliminate that risk.

No platform intermediary — direct contracts, direct communication, direct accountability.
Stable part numbers — LIA codes are permanent references that don't change with market conditions.
Audit-ready documentation — all specs, certs, and QC records are archived and available on request, indefinitely.
Multi-supplier redundancy — each SKU category has 2–3 qualified production sources to protect your delivery schedule.

After-Sales Support

Local Accountability. Not Long-Distance Excuses.

When a batch has a dimensional issue, you need a resolution — not a timezone delay. LIAPLIAS maintains a European-facing support structure with clear escalation protocols and documented response commitments.

✓

Quality claim response within 24 hours — not 5 business days. Root cause analysis within 72 hours for confirmed non-conformances.

✓

Replacement or credit within one delivery cycle — for confirmed defective batches, we resolve at our cost, not yours.

LIA traceability system — every batch number links to factory, production date, QC report, and material certificate. Investigation starts with data, not guesswork.

✓

Dedicated account contact — one person who knows your specifications, your history, and your delivery requirements. Not a rotating ticket queue.

03
Key Account Development

Built for Customers Who Buy at Scale.

For purchasing managers and procurement teams with regular, high-volume component needs, LIAPLIAS offers a structured key account program — with consolidated pricing, scheduled delivery, and dedicated technical support to manage your entire standard component portfolio.

✓

Annual framework agreements — fixed pricing for 12 months, with volume-tiered discounts. Budget predictability for your procurement cycle.

✓

Portfolio consolidation — we map your existing HASCO or Meusburger parts to LIA equivalents and provide a single consolidated catalog for your standard components.

✓

Scheduled replenishment — for high-frequency SKUs, we set up buffer stock at our side and trigger shipments on your schedule, not on a reactive basis.

✓

Technical consultation included — our team reviews your tooling BOM and identifies consolidation opportunities, alternative specifications, or cost reduction options.

What Key Account Customers Get

A structured long-term supply relationship — not ad hoc ordering. Designed for procurement teams managing 50–500+ component SKUs across multiple tooling projects.

Fixed annual pricing — no spot-market volatility. Your unit costs are contractually set for the agreement period.
BOM management service — we maintain your component catalog in the LIA system and flag obsolete or out-of-spec items proactively.
Priority production slots — key account orders are scheduled before spot orders at our factories. Lead time is guaranteed, not estimated.
Quarterly business review — usage analysis, new product introductions, and pricing review every quarter. Supply chain as a managed partnership.
Customs & compliance support — CE documentation, import duty classification, and REACH/RoHS compliance records maintained for your audit files.
