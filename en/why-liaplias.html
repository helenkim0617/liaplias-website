Why LIAPLIAS

One Process.
One Point of Contact.
Full Accountability.

LIAPLIAS works with a network of qualified manufacturing partners to receive, review, and deliver on standard component and custom manufacturing requirements. Each requirement is reviewed, matched to the appropriate manufacturing capability, and followed through to delivery under a single point of contact.

Our Model

Built on Three Commitments

Choosing a manufacturing partner is not about finding a factory — it's about identifying the right manufacturing path for your requirement, and the right partner within our network to deliver it.

⚙️

Engineered to Standard

Standard components are built to established dimensions and tolerances, including HASCO and Meusburger compatibility. Custom requirements are reviewed against your specifications.

🔗

Manufacturing Capability Matched to Your Requirements

Requirements are matched to the appropriate manufacturing capability within our network of qualified manufacturing partners. The appropriate capability can vary by quantity, project stage, cost target and lead time, so this matching is reviewed for each order rather than fixed to a single supplier.

Every batch is assigned a traceable lot number, supported by production and inspection records.

🤝

One Contact, Full Accountability

A single point of contact coordinates your project from requirement review through delivery, without added layers of communication or hand-offs.

The Process

From Inquiry to Delivery

01

Receive & Review

Submit drawings, specifications, BOMs, or code-based inquiries. We confirm the scope before matching the requirement to manufacturing capability.

02

Engineering Review & Quotation

We review the available technical information, including materials and tolerances where applicable, before issuing a quotation.

03

Sample & Approval

Where applicable, samples, prototypes, or first-article validation are inspected against specification, and production proceeds only after approval.

04

Production & QC

Production is monitored and QC inspection is completed before shipment. Each batch is assigned a traceable lot number with supporting production and inspection records.

05

Export & Delivery

Export documentation, shipment planning, and delivery coordination are handled according to project requirements.

06

After-Sales & Reorder

Approved specifications and manufacturing assignments are retained for your account, supporting more direct reorders.

02
Platform Independence

Built for Long-Term Manufacturing Continuity

LIAPLIAS is structured around a direct, ongoing manufacturing relationship — not an open marketplace or transaction-only model. Orders, specifications, and commercial terms are maintained directly with us to support continuity over time.

✓

Direct Commercial Relationship — you deal with LIAPLIAS directly, without a third-party marketplace intermediary or changing platform terms.

✓

Specification Continuity — approved specifications, material grades, and manufacturing assignments are maintained for consistency across orders.

✓

Transparent Pricing — unit price, tooling cost, and freight are itemized separately, without bundled fees.

🔗

Why Continuity Matters

A direct, ongoing relationship reduces the need to restart sourcing, clarify specifications, or rebuild project information with every order.

No Platform Intermediary — direct communication and accountability through LIAPLIAS.
Documentation Continuity — specifications, certificates, and QC records are archived and available on request.
Project Continuity — technical information and manufacturing assignments support reorders and future projects.

03
After-Sales Support

Response & Resolution Framework

When quality issues arise, LIAPLIAS provides a documented response process with clear escalation and follow-up.

✓

24-Hour Claim Response — quality claims receive a response within 24 hours, followed by root cause analysis for confirmed non-conformances.

✓

Corrective Action — confirmed non-conformances are addressed through replacement, credit, or corrective action, coordinated directly with your account contact, based on the specifics of the issue.

✓

Data-Driven Investigation — each batch can be traced through its production date, QC report, and material certificate, allowing investigations to start with documented data.

✓

Dedicated Account Contact — one contact coordinates the response and knows your specifications, order history, and delivery requirements.

04
Key Account Development

Structured for Recurring Manufacturing Programs

For ongoing production, multi-part BOMs, or recurring manufacturing programs, LIAPLIAS provides structured support beyond individual purchase orders across commercial, technical, and production requirements.

✓

Framework Agreements — for recurring requirements, framework agreements can support ongoing sourcing, production planning, and commercial coordination beyond individual purchase orders.

✓

BOM & Specification Management — BOMs, drawings, specifications, and approved revisions are maintained as part of the account history, reducing repeated clarification across subsequent orders.

✓

Ongoing Technical Support — LIAPLIAS coordinates technical clarification, specification updates, and manufacturing requirements with the relevant manufacturing partners as requirements evolve.

✓

Production Planning & Continuity — recurring production is coordinated according to project requirements and agreed schedules. Where appropriate, inventory or buffer-stock arrangements can also be discussed.

📋

Program-Level Support

Recurring manufacturing programs require coordinated commercial, technical, and production support beyond individual orders.

Commercial Coordination — framework agreements support ongoing sourcing and production planning.
BOM & Revision Management — maintained technical information supports subsequent orders.
Production Coordination — recurring requirements are planned around agreed project schedules.
